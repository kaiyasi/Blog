## Cross-Border Synergy and the Essence of Data

Before the official launch of the activities, the pre-event orientation on basic tool usage did feel somewhat out of place for a computer science student like myself. That sense of being guided as a beginner gradually transformed into an understanding of cross-national collaboration after meeting my Korean peers. Despite being an introverted person, the language barrier made initial communication feel silent, but we quickly found a common language in the digital world. Through text exchanges on Discord and Google Chat, I was amazed by the profound programming logic of my Korean junior who was only a ninth grader. This technical resonance that transcended age and national borders was more convincing than any words could be. After Prof. JP’s ice-breaking opening dance that challenged cultural conventions, we focused our project on a weighty issue facing both Taiwan and Korea: traffic and air pollution under high population density. Through extracting government open data and map visualization techniques, we attempted to find solutions to environmental challenges in the flow of data. This was not merely a practice in data analysis, but a thoughtful process of empowering technology with social care, teaching us how to read the city’s breath from cold numbers.

## Professional Vistas and the Weight of Collaboration

Walking into Google’s office on the 77th floor of Taipei 101 on the third day, the vast sensation of overlooking the horizon intertwined with my previous experience visiting Amazon, forming a concrete aspiration for my future career. Conversations with engineers on-site redefined my understanding of the threshold to multinational enterprises, revealing that fluent communication and problem-solving abilities often hold more value than sophisticated terminology. However, the deepest shock from this activity occurred on the final presentation stage. When I became complacent because my junior voluntarily took on the report, the silence on stage and the sudden speech stumble instantly awakened me to my dereliction of duty. In that impromptu rescue, I realized that there should be no bystanders in team collaboration—every deliverable carries the success or failure of the whole. This experience not only filled the regret of missing international exchanges in the past but also made me see my responsibilities and potential beyond technology. Moving forward, I will carry this passion for big data and AI fields, continuously refining efficiency and creativity in information presentation, hoping to enter this challenging technological realm with greater maturity in the near future.
